Featured Visionaries
Doug Chiang

Academy Award-winning production designer and the visual architect of Star Wars, Doug Chiang was the first artist Frank Beddor brought to The Looking Glass Wars.

He didn't just imagine Wonderland.
He made it believable.

Chris Appelhans

Academy Award-winning director of KPop Demon Hunters, Chris Appelhans gave LGW one of its most haunting images: a teenage Rose Heart, years before she became Queen Redd.

He drew the girl before the catastrophe.

Vincent Proce

Concept artist on The Shape of Water, Vincent Proce painted the image of Hatter Madigan that stopped strangers in their tracks at Comic-Con and became the defining portrait of Wonderland's most beloved bodyguard.

Some images define a franchise. This is one of them.

The Sounds of Wonder
When the heart can no longer speak… it sings.

The Looking Glass Wars is a sweeping cinematic musical that reveals the history hidden inside one of the world's most beloved stories. Set across Victorian England and the breathtaking civilization of Wonderland, it follows Alyss, a young woman forced to rediscover the truth of who she is before she can reclaim the Queendom she was born to lead. At once an epic adventure and an intimate coming-of-age journey, it explores the questions every generation must answer—identity, belonging, courage, love, and the power of choosing your own path. Through breathtaking theatrical spectacle, unforgettable characters, and a soaring contemporary score, The Looking Glass Wars transforms a familiar classic into an original mythology for a new generation, asking one of the defining questions of our time: Who gets to decide what's real?

Meet the Team
Shaun McKenna

An award-winning English screenwriter, playwright and lyricist. McKenna is considered prolific and versatile, known for both original dramas and adaptations. His output ranges from screenplays to prime-time British TV, from large-scale arena spectaculars to straight plays, and from West End musicals to BBC radio and documentaries. A few of his credits include: The Lord of the Rings – A Musical Tale, Maddie, Heidi, Lautrec, La Cava, Rocky Road, and Return of Peter Pan.

Pippa Cleary

An English musical theatre composer, songwriter, lyricist and singer. She is the only female British composer to have had three musicals produced in the West End and was mentored by Claude-Michel Schönberg. A few of her credits include: Regards to Broadway, The Great British Bake Off: The Musical, My Son's A Queer (But What Can You Do?), The Secret Diary of Adrian Mole Aged 13¾, In the Willows, and A Midsummer Night's Dream – The Musical.
