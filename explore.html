EXPLORE WONDERLAND

Every civilization is defined by its institutions, its landscapes, and the places where history is made.

Heart Palace
The Heart of the Queendom

The seat of the Heart Dynasty and home to the Heart Crystal, Heart Palace is the political and symbolic center of Wonderland. Behind its soaring architecture, singing gardens, and royal grandeur lies a world of diplomacy, espionage, and constant intrigue. Every decision made here shapes the future of the Queendom.

House of Cards
Where Power Is Dealt

Wonderland's military and political command center. Here, strategy is forged, alliances rise and fall, and decks of Card Soldiers stand ready to be dealt into battle. Every kingdom has a seat of power. Wonderland built one from cards.

The Crystal Continuum
Wonderland's Living Transit System

A vast network of Looking Glasses connects every corner of Wonderland. Mastering the Crystal Continuum is an art, allowing skilled travelers to move across the Queendom in moments. More than transportation, it is the civilization's greatest engineering achievement.

Valley of Mushrooms
Home of the Caterpillar Oracles

Hidden beneath towering mushrooms and twilight skies, the Valley is where Wonderland's ancient Caterpillar Oracles weave the Seven Threads. Here, prophecy, wisdom, and imagination have guided the Queendom for generations.

Everlasting Forest
Wonderland's Living Wilderness

Stretching across the heart of the continent, the Everlasting Forest supplies the Queendom with rare resources, extraordinary creatures, and endless challenges. It is both a classroom and a proving ground, where Milliner Cadets and Alchemists learn that survival begins with understanding nature—not conquering it.

Wondertropolis
The Capital of Imagination

The capital of Wonderland is where centuries of history meet impossible innovation. Quartz-like towers crowned with shimmering pyrite rise above ancient streets, while sleek glass transit glides silently through the city on cushions of air. Every district reflects the civilization that built it—part royal capital, part technological marvel, part living work of art.

Wondertropolis isn't simply where Wonderland's government resides. It's the beating heart of an extraordinary civilization.

THE FOUR FAMILIES

Winning a throne is one thing. Governing a Queendom is another.

The Hearts may occupy Wonderland’s throne.

They do not own Wonderland.

For generations, four great Suit families have competed for influence across the Queendom:

♥Hearts
♠Spades
♣Clubs
♦Diamonds

Each has its own history.

Its own strengths.

Its own ambitions.

Its own relationship with power.

Hearts have ruled.

Spades make exceptional spies

Clubs value strength and independence.

Diamonds understand commerce, influence and control.

And beneath alliances forged in public are old resentments that don’t disappear simply because a war has been won.

This becomes one of the great engines of Alyss’s life after Redd.

She can defeat an enemy.

She cannot defeat politics.

The young princess who once fought to get home must become a Queen capable of holding together people who don’t always agree on what Wonderland should become.

Rival Suit families challenge her.

Political alliances shift.

Attempts are made on her life.

Beyond Wonderland’s borders, other rulers see opportunity.

And every compromise Alyss makes to preserve peace creates consequences of its own.

The throne turns out to be another battlefield.

House of Cards

THE HOUSE OF CARDS

Every hand is dealt from here.

At the center of Wonderland’s military power stands the House of Cards.

Not a metaphor.

An institution.

Card Soldiers belong to the four Suits and possess different strengths, specialties and loyalties. They can be assembled into Hands designed for specific missions and dealt into battle at the Queen’s discretion.

War in Wonderland isn’t fought by anonymous armies. The hand matters: who you choose, which Suits you combine, what abilities they possess, and whether they trust one another.

♥Hearts

A Heart may win the battle before the first Card Soldier is dealt.

Or become so certain of the strategy that they fail to see the move they never anticipated.

♠Spades

A Spade may possess precisely the intelligence a mission requires.

Unless the Spade is spying on you.

♣Clubs

A Club may be the warrior who saves the Hand.

Or the one who starts the fight everyone was trying to avoid.

♦Diamonds

A Diamond may negotiate the alliance that prevents a war.

Or decide the alliance is worth more broken.

The House of Cards is part Pentagon, part intelligence service, part weapons laboratory—the place where Wonderland prepares for the threats it knows are coming and invents answers to threats it hasn’t imagined yet.

Every Card Soldier belongs to a Suit.

Every Suit belongs to a family.

Which means even Wonderland’s army carries its politics into battle.
